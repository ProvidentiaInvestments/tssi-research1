2. Business Description
Company Overview

TSS, Inc. (TSSI) is a pure-play AI data center infrastructure services company operating from a single purpose-built facility in Georgetown, Texas. The company provides three services to its near-exclusive customer, Dell Technologies: Systems Integration (rack assembly and liquid cooling deployment), Procurement Services (component sourcing and logistics), and Facilities Management (ongoing operational support).

The defining structural characteristic is the consigned component model: Dell retains ownership of all bill-of-materials components, with TSSI earning a service fee for integration labour, cleanroom operations, and liquid cooling expertise. This eliminates component price risk entirely and creates a predictable, margin-stable revenue stream in the high-margin SI segment.

TSSI is, in essence, the entity that physically assembles Dell's most complex AI server infrastructure. Its Georgetown facility is a licensed cleanroom environment with direct-liquid-cooling (DLC) capability that took approximately 18 months and $90M+ of combined capex to establish.

The Georgetown Facility

The Georgetown, Texas facility is TSSI's primary competitive asset and its most significant barrier to entry. Established across FY2023-FY2024, it represents approximately $90M of combined capex and was designed specifically to support Dell's next-generation liquid-cooled AI server programmes.

Key characteristics include a licensed cleanroom environment required for GPU-density rack assembly, Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC) infrastructure for AI workloads above 100kW per rack, and proximity to Dell's Round Rock HQ (~35 miles) enabling tight operational coordination.

The $17M FY2026E capex adds next-generation rack compatibility for Blackwell-class GPU deployments, extending Georgetown's capacity to serve anticipated SI volume growth through FY2027 and beyond.

The Dell Relationship

Dell Technologies constitutes approximately 99% of TSSI's revenue, a concentration that is simultaneously the company's greatest risk and its most powerful competitive moat. The relationship is governed by a Master Services Agreement most recently amended in December 2025, which extended the contract by two years and included a service rate increase that directly elevated SI gross margins from ~22% to ~38%.

The structural depth of the relationship extends beyond the contract. TSSI's Chief Sales Officer and Chief Technology Officer are both former Dell Technologies executives, a deliberate hiring pattern that reflects the degree to which TSSI has been architected to serve Dell's specific technical and operational requirements. The Georgetown facility was co-designed with Dell's infrastructure team and has been physically integrated into Dell's AI server supply chain in a manner that makes substitution operationally disruptive and expensive.

The switching cost is not merely contractual; it is physical. Replacing TSSI would require Dell to build equivalent cleanroom and DLC infrastructure internally (12-18 month lead time, $50M+ capex) or qualify a new third-party integrator for Blackwell-class hardware (6-12 months minimum).

The December 2025 contract extension, made with full knowledge of TSSI's prior operational disruption, reflects Dell's revealed preference for continuity. That choice, by a $90B revenue company with access to many integration alternatives, is itself the most powerful mitigant to concentration risk available.

E - Environmental

The environmental picture for TSSI is structurally nuanced and requires careful framing. The Georgetown facility operates at up to 40 MW of electrical capacity - a material power consumption footprint for a company of TSSI's scale. No Scope 1, 2, or 3 emissions data has been published. Carbon transition risk is not currently material given TSSI's service-based business model (no direct manufacturing or fossil fuel exposure), but the absence of disclosure creates a potential ESG-screen exclusion risk as institutional ownership standards evolve.

The DLC efficiency argument: Direct liquid cooling is materially more energy-efficient than air cooling at equivalent AI compute density. A data centre running Blackwell-generation racks at 132 kW per rack via DLC consumes significantly less total energy than an equivalent compute deployment attempting air cooling at lower density. TSSI's core service capability - enabling DLC at scale - is therefore net positive for the energy efficiency of AI infrastructure broadly, even as the Georgetown facility itself consumes substantial power.

Forward recommendation: Initiating a voluntary Scope 2 disclosure programme and publishing a basic sustainability framework would materially reduce ESG-screen exclusion risk at minimal cost. This is a straightforward governance action the new CSO and CTO are well-positioned to drive given their institutional backgrounds.

S - Social

TSSI's social profile is early-stage and not currently a material investment consideration. The Georgetown facility represents a meaningful skilled manufacturing employer in Williamson County, Texas - a mid-sized community where the economic multiplier effect of a growing high-tech manufacturing operation is significant. As SI volumes scale and the workforce expands, the social footprint will grow proportionally.

No published data is available on workforce diversity, safety incident rates, employee turnover, or supply chain labour practices. The consigned component model limits TSSI's direct supply chain labour exposure - component sourcing decisions sit with Dell Technologies, not TSSI. This structural characteristic reduces social supply chain risk relative to peers that own their bill of materials.

G - Governance

Governance is TSSI's strongest ESG pillar and one of the more compelling qualitative arguments for the investment thesis. Management has demonstrated a consistent pattern of conservative guidance, proactive disclosure of unfavourable one-time items, and capital allocation decisions tied directly to contractually secured returns rather than speculative growth investment.
